Why no amount of reward/critic tuning fixes this: single-action leverage
========================================================================
2026-09-19. scripts-equivalent: scratchpad/action_leverage.py

MOTIVATION. EV saturated at 1.000, so EV_f was added as the honest diagnostic.
After DIRT_COST made the objective non-empty, EV_f ALSO saturated at 1.000 by
update 136. Two diagnostics dead the same way is a hint that the thing being
measured is not critic quality. So measure the underlying quantity directly.

METHOD. Warm a floor to mid-job (step 1500). From that ONE fixed state, take
K=64 different first actions, then follow the SAME scripted policy for H=300
steps (60 s). The env is deterministic given (state, action), so ALL spread in
the resulting discounted GENUINE return is attributable to that single action.
This is, in expectation, exactly what PPO's advantage estimates.

RESULT.
  discounted genuine return (60 s) : -111.8905
  std across 64 first actions      :    0.006254   <- one action
  full range                       : [-111.9065, -111.8779]  (spread 0.0285)
  std / |mean|                     :    5.589e-05

  std across 8 different FLOORS,
  same policy, same horizon        :    3.375112

  ONE ACTION / WHOLE FLOOR         :    0.0019

READING. The action-attributable signal is 0.19% of the nuisance variation
between floors -- a ~500:1 noise-to-signal ratio at the batch level, before
any question of reward design. PPO must pick the first number out of the
second.

WHAT THIS EXPLAINS.
  * EV and EV_f both -> 1.000. The critic is not failing; it is correctly
    predicting the 99.8% of return set by which floor was drawn and how far
    through the job the reset landed. The residual it cannot predict is the
    0.19%, which is the only part that carries action information.
  * Advantage normalisation cannot help. `adv = (adv - mean)/std` rescales a
    noise-dominated quantity to unit variance; it amplifies the 500:1 noise
    exactly as faithfully as it would amplify signal.
  * Why every knob was a null. Five configurations spanning alpha 0/0.5/1 and
    a 1000x range of ent_coef all landed at fraction_clean 0.354-0.364. After
    DIRT_COST, three more landed at 0.34-0.44. The knobs were never the
    binding constraint.

WHAT IT DOES NOT SAY. It does not say the simulation is wrong, and it does not
say the reward fixes were wrong -- DIRT_COST did exactly what it claimed
(r_gen std 0.000000 -> 0.021, advantages stopped collapsing). It says the
CONTROL PROBLEM AS FRAMED has almost no per-decision leverage: 4500 sequential
choices, each worth 5.6e-5 of the outcome.

CONSISTENT WITH THE PHYSICS. Independently measured, coverage gaps outweigh
genuinely-uncuttable grit 3-4:1 by mass, and in one seed 98% of the leftover
grit had simply never been walked over. The floor is cleaned by WHERE YOU WALK,
not by what the wand does in any given 0.2 s. Both measurements say the same
thing from opposite ends.

IMPLICATION. If RL is to be worth further spend here, the action space is the
thing to change, not the reward. A policy choosing lane spacing, sweep
direction, segment length and when to cross to the far side would make a
handful of decisions per episode, each worth a large fraction of the outcome --
the exact inverse of the current 4500-decisions-at-5.6e-5 arrangement. That is
a redesign, and it should be costed against the fact that the scripted sweep
already answers Andrew's practical question at 0.722 clean.

CONFIRMED BY THE ent_coef=0 RUN (same day, alpha=1.0 both).
  fixed-a10   ent_coef 0.003   final entropy 6.157   clean 0.441
  fixed-ent0  ent_coef 0.0     final entropy 4.273   clean 0.450
A 1.9-NAT difference in entropy -- the bonus removed entirely, drift reversed,
policy sigma held near its initialisation instead of inflating to ~0.8 -- buys
0.009 of clean. Third independent confirmation that entropy is a passenger
here, and a direct prediction of the leverage result: a knob cannot matter when
the action-attributable signal is 0.19% of the batch noise.

SCOREBOARD OF THE REWARD FIXES (fresh floor, training-time fraction_clean).
  before any fix (5-run ablation, final)   0.354 - 0.364
  after gamma + DIRT_COST + fresh eval     0.441 - 0.450
  hand-coded far_to_near, 30 min           0.722
The fixes are worth a real +0.08, and they removed a genuine bug. They do not
close the gap to the scripted sweep, and the leverage number above says why.
