Five-run GPU ablation: why the policy plateaus at ~0.39 clean
=============================================================
2026-09-19. 5x RTX-class pods, 600 updates requested, read at 130-160.
ent0030/ent0003/ent0000 vary ent_coef at alpha=1. alpha000/alpha050 vary
potential_baseline_alpha at ent_coef=0.003. All other config identical.

ARM 1 -- ent_coef (0.003 / 0.0003 / 0.0), alpha=1
  run       ent_coef   entropy 4.59 ->   clean upd3 -> upd159   adv_std
  ent0030   0.003      4.59 -> 4.656 UP   0.363 -> 0.387        0.99 -> 0.433
  ent0003   0.0003     4.59 -> 4.421 dn   0.363 -> 0.411        0.99 -> 0.443
  ent0000   0.0        4.59 -> 4.415 dn   0.363 -> 0.378        0.99 -> 0.403

  ent_coef controls entropy exactly as advertised: it rises at 0.003 and falls
  at 0.0003 and 0.0. fraction_clean is IDENTICAL across all three (0.378-0.411,
  inside seed spread). Removing the entropy bonus entirely rescues nothing.
  ENTROPY IS A PASSENGER, NOT A CAUSE.

ARM 2 -- potential_baseline_alpha
  alpha000: EV 0.0003 -> 0.9996 over 131 updates. adv_std 7.89 -> 1.05.
            clean 0.280 -> 0.426 (peak upd 71) -> 0.388 (upd 131).
  alpha=1 (ent0030): EV = 1.0000 from update 1. clean flat 0.39 throughout.

  This is the mechanism in one curve. With alpha=0 the critic starts ignorant
  of Phi, so the shaping is NOT absorbed, advantages are large (7.9), and the
  policy improves (0.28 -> 0.426). As the critic learns Phi (EV -> 0.9996) the
  shaping gets absorbed, advantages collapse (-> 1.05), and the policy stops
  improving and DECAYS BACK to the same 0.39 plateau.

  Potential-based shaping supplies policy gradient only while the critic is
  WRONG about Phi. Once EV -> 1 it is inert. alpha only sets WHEN that happens:
  alpha=1 starts absorbed (no learning ever), alpha=0 absorbs over ~70 updates
  and buys one transient bump. Neither endpoint works -- as pre-registered.

WHY: THE UNSHAPED OBJECTIVE IS EMPTY
  1. r_gen (reward minus shaping) is CONSTANT: -0.2000/step, std 0.000000,
     one unique value over 3000 steps. It is just -TIME_COST.
  2. finished_clean = 0.0 on EVERY update of all five runs. The FINISH_BONUS
     has fired ZERO times in >160M steps. worst_residual RISES (0.050 -> 0.062
     -0.088), so completion is not being approached.
  => The unshaped MDP's return is identical for every policy. Its advantages
     are identically zero. At alpha=1 PPO computes exactly those advantages,
     which is why the signal is exactly zero rather than merely weak.

  Confirmed in closed form: at alpha=1 the learned head f = value_mean +
  200*phi_mean must converge to r_gen/(1-gamma) = -0.2/0.001 = -200.0.
  Measured, identically in all three ent runs:
     upd    1   f =    0.4   (0% -- zeroed head at init, as designed)
     upd   40   f =  -87.8   (44%)
     upd  100   f = -105.1   (53%)
     upd  159   f = -112.9   (56%)
  40M steps of training to get 56% of the way to a constant computable by hand.
  That is the critic's entire job in this configuration.

SECOND, INDEPENDENT DEFECT: HORIZON MISMATCH
  gamma = 0.999 -> effective horizon 1/(1-gamma) = 1000 steps = 3.3 min.
  Episode = 4500 steps = 15.0 min. The agent cannot see the end of its episode.
  gamma^4500 = 0.0111, so FINISH_BONUS=400 is worth 4.43 reward units at
  episode start, against -200 of accumulated time cost over the same horizon
  -- a ratio of 0.022. Even if completion were reachable it would be invisible.
     gamma=0.9995 -> horizon  2000 steps ( 6.7 min), g^4500 = 0.105
     gamma=0.9998 -> horizon  5000 steps (16.7 min), g^4500 = 0.407
     gamma=0.9999 -> horizon 10000 steps (33.3 min), g^4500 = 0.638

CONCLUSION
  Not a tuning problem, and not fixable by any value of alpha or ent_coef. The
  reward is a sparse terminal-reward problem whose terminal event is
  unreachable and, if reached, discounted to ~1%. The dense shaping made this
  look like a working dense-reward problem; it was masking an empty objective.
  The apparent learning in T1 was the transient in the alpha000 curve above.

  EV=1.0000 was never a sign of a healthy critic. It reports that the analytic
  offset explains all target variance. EV_f (0.16-0.38, not trending up) is the
  honest number and should be the one on the dashboard.
